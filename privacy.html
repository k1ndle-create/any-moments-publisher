Any Moments Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Information processed

The application may process account identifiers, OAuth tokens, local videos, metadata, upload history and operational logs to authenticate accounts, manage uploads and prevent duplicates.

Storage and sharing

These records are stored locally. Authorized videos and necessary metadata are transmitted to supported platforms. TikTok integration is planned, not currently implemented. The application is not designed to sell personal information or use it for advertising.

YouTube use is subject to the YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Users may delete local application records and revoke access through platform settings, including Google account permissions. Published content is managed through the relevant platform.

Rights and security

Users may request access, correction or deletion as applicable. Protect local credentials and computer access. No storage method guarantees absolute security.
